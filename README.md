# Study_Clock
Study Clock is a smart study timer that automatically tracks your study hours using Start and Stop buttons. It calculates daily and weekly study time, saves your sessions, and generates progress reports to help you study smarter.

Open `study_timer.html` to use the timer. Choose **Your status** to open `study_status.html`, where you can review daily study hours and select a day to see study times by hour.

## Offline use

Open the published app once while online so its pages and chart library are cached on the device. Install it from the browser menu (Chrome/Edge: **Install app** or **Add to Home screen**; iPhone/iPad Safari: **Share > Add to Home Screen**). After that, sessions are saved in that browser on that device and the timer and status dashboard can open offline. Google Drive sync requires an internet connection and is optional.
